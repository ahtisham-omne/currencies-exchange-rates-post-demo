# Sales Orders: currency & exchange-rate touchpoints

> **27 Nov 2026:** "Mid-market" is now the **daily market rate** everywhere (Eli's ruling, 26 Nov 2026: one rate per currency per day, read once a day from Open Exchange Rates and fixed for that date). This record uses the new name; the code keeps the identifier `mid`. Mahreen's design changes of the same day are in [currency-review-27-nov-plan.md](currency-review-27-nov-plan.md).

Sales Orders was redesigned. The new app comes from [`ahtisham-omne/Sales-Order-V5`](https://github.com/ahtisham-omne/Sales-Order-V5), at commit `a3a5940` (5 Nov 2026). This record does three things:

1. Lists the currency touchpoints the earlier Sales Orders touchpoint had (§1).
2. Maps each one onto the redesign (§2).
3. Says where everything is defined now (§3–§6), including the base-currency view, the toggle, the currency configuration and the version rate choice brought back afterwards (§3a), and the Currency Touchpoints Map's changes, which removed the toggle again (§3c).

The redesign replaced the earlier touchpoint in `touchpoints/sales-order` as a squashed git subtree. The slug, port (8103) and mount (`/sales`) are unchanged.

Sources:

- *Currencies & Exchange Rates: where each module changes* (design touchpoint audit, 4 Nov 2026), Sales Orders, pages 13–14: "Inherits currency and rate from the quote, or from the customer when created directly." Five changes, from spec 4.3 and 4.4.
- *Currency & Exchange Rates Functional Spec v1.1*, sections 4.3 and 4.4 (as the audit cites them).

## 3a. Base currency first, the toggle, the configuration and version rates (18 Nov 2026)

Asked for after the audit's changes, as for Purchase Orders ("In POs, the prices should always be in the base currency. And on the hover, it should show vendor currency if different. Add the toggle and currency configuration for PO. … add these for versioning"), with "Same pointers for Sales Orders & Quotes". This brings back the earlier touchpoint's model (§1) on the redesign's data, which is unchanged: an order's amounts are still kept in its currency with a rate to base, so what changes is what's shown. Paths are under `touchpoints/sales-order/src/app/`.

- **Base by default.** Every amount on the order is shown in PKR, the customer's amount and the rate in the hover (`components/currency/Money.tsx` `BaseMoney` / `BaseMoneyCard`: the base currency on top, "In PKR", "Customer currency", the rate, its type and day). On the SO page the rate is the order's; creating an order uses today's. The view sits on the document-currency context (`docCurrencyContext.ts` `view`, `docInBase`, `useDocView`, `useDocMoney`, `useDocWhole`, `useDocOtherText`, `useDesignMoney`; `DocCurrency.tsx` `DocMoney`; `useRuleMoney.ts` `money` / `baseText`), so line rows, Order Summary, invoicing, linked documents, hover cards, the rule, adjustment and freight modals, the line configurator and the release, cancel and archive confirmations all follow it.
- **The toggle and the Currency button.** The SO header has Base Currency | Customer Currency (only on an order in a foreign currency) and a Currency button, before the Full Screen and Actions buttons (`components/currency/SOCurrencyHeaderControls.tsx`, placed in `SODetail.tsx`). The view is kept per order for the session (`components/currency/soView.ts`) and is never an edit or a version change.
- **Prices typed in the shown currency.** In base view the price-per-unit capsule takes PKR; the price is converted at the order's rate and rounded to the order currency's minor unit (`SOLineGrid.tsx` `PriceCapsule`). An untouched input keeps the stored price. Line totals and the price breakdown hover are in the shown currency, with the other one under the breakdown's Total.
- **Order Summary** keeps the Grand Total in the shown currency (the other one on hover and in the caption) and, after the breakdown, the CURRENCY card (`SOCurrencyConfig.tsx` `SOCurrencySummary`): the rate-type chip, a Locked chip once the order leaves Draft, Customer pays / You receive / Base equivalent, the rate(s) and View configuration.
- **Currency Configuration** (`SOCurrencyConfig.tsx` `SOCurrencyConfigDialog`, rendered by `SOSidebar.tsx`): Customer pays (the order's currency; changing it converts every line, through the same confirmation as before), You receive (the settlement currency, PKR unless set; `SalesOrder.settlementCurrency`), the rate type and a corporate rate that can be changed with a reset to finance's, the rate cards, the amber lock note, and Reset to default. As the inline fields were, it can be changed while the order is a Draft and is read-only (Locked) after; a change is logged in Activity.
- **Versions.** Starting a version (`AutoVersionConfirmModal.tsx`, from a material change on a Released, Partially Shipped or Partially Cancelled order, "Edit as V{n}", and reverts from the Versions tab and the version dropdown) and reverting from the listing's or board's Version History (`VersionHistoryModal.tsx`) ask, on a foreign order, "Use locked rate from V{n}" or "Apply current rate" (`components/currency/VersionRateChoice.tsx`). Applying it restamps the order with today's rate of its type (`so/soCurrency.ts` `currentRateFor` / `withCurrentRate`; `store.tsx` `createVersionDraft` / `revertToVersion` take the choice) and logs an "Exchange rate Changed" entry in Activity; the customer's amounts don't move, the base totals do. Each version records the rate it took (`SOVersion` currency fields, `stampVersionRate`), so a revert with the locked rate restores V{n}'s (`versionRateFields`). The Versions tab and the Version History dialog show base at each version's own rate, or the order's currency when the page is toggled; compare adds an Exchange rate row.
- **Outside the SO page** amounts are base at the order's rate: the listing's Order Value, Invoiced Amount and Payments Made, the board's cards, the bulk release and submit modals, and modals opened from a row (wrapped in `SODocCurrency`). Creating an order shows catalog prices, line totals and the subtotal in base at today's rate, the customer's figure on hover and under the subtotal (`CreateSOStep2.tsx`). The listing's totals were already in base and are unchanged.
- **Not changed:** the printed order and its PDF (`PrintSalesOrder.tsx`, `SendToCustomerModal.tsx`, `soDocumentData.ts`) stay in the order's currency, the customer's document; so do the export file, the import preview, the change notes in version history and Activity (what the order recorded), and inputs labelled with the order's currency (one-time adjustment amounts, freight, the configurator's price).

## 3c. Currency Touchpoints Map (24 Nov 2026)

The product team's *Currency Touchpoints Map*, "Quote to sales order" (1: converting a quote, done in Quotes; 2: the sales order after conversion), and its pricing-rule items, which apply to every module with its own copy of the rules. Where it differs from an earlier decision the map wins; the conflicts are listed at the end. The map's figures (SO-000-112, $14,280.00, 276.1000) are illustrations: the demo keeps its own orders and rates. Paths are under `touchpoints/sales-order/src/app/`.

- **No toggle; a currency tag in the header.** The Base Currency | Customer Currency toggle is gone and the order is always shown in base, the customer's amount and the rate on hover. `so/SODetail.tsx` pins the view (`soView = "base"`) and keeps the `DocCurrencyProvider` plumbing; `components/currency/soView.ts` is removed. Line 1 of the header, after the state pills, reads "from Q-… · {customer} [USD]" (`components/currency/SOCurrencyHeaderControls.tsx` `SOHeaderCustomer`): the quote the order came from, the customer and, on an order in a foreign currency, its `CurrencyPill`. A PKR order gets no tag. The Currency button stays where it was, before Full Screen and Actions (`SOCurrencyHeaderControls`).
- **Listing and board.** A foreign-currency order carries the same tag after the customer's name (`so/SOList.tsx` Customer cell, `so/SOKanbanBoard.tsx` card). Its amounts were already in base with the hover; PKR orders stay plain.
- **Order Summary.** Subtotal, Pricing Rules Applied and One-Time Discounts/Premiums now have the hover the Grand Total and the invoice rows have (`so/SOSidebar.tsx`, `DocMoney`). The CURRENCY block (`components/currency/SOCurrencyConfig.tsx` `SOCurrencySummary`): the rate-type chip and a lock chip that is always there on a foreign order, Unlocked (amber, `LockOpen`) on a Draft and Locked (green) once confirmed, its tooltip saying when it locks; Customer pays / You receive (always, in the account's currency) / Base equivalent (only when the account isn't in base); a hairline, then "Rate · 1 USD = ₨278.5200 PKR"; View configuration. A PKR order keeps Customer pays and "PKR is the base currency, so there's no conversion."
- **"You receive" is an account.** `currency/companyAccounts.ts` copies the Vendor Invoicing `PAY_FROM_ACCOUNTS` entries the map names, with the same ids: 1010 JPMorgan Operating, 1020 Corporate Checking, 1030 AP Disbursements and 1000 Petty Cash Drawer in PKR; 1040 Global FX Settlement (USD), 1050 EUR, 1060 GBP and 1070 AED Settlement. An order is received into one in PKR or in its own currency (`eligibleAccounts`); the default is 1010. The choice is `SalesOrder.payToAccount`, and `settlementCurrency` is that account's currency (`so/soCurrency.ts` `soReceiveAccount`, `withSOCurrency`, `soRateFields`). An order saved with a bare "You receive" currency resolves to the first account in that currency when it can take it, else 1010 (`resolveReceiveAccount`). Versions record the account with the rate (`SOVersion.payToAccount`, `versionRateFields`); a duplicate for the same customer keeps it (`so/duplicateSO.ts`). CAD and JPY orders have no account in their currency, so they are received in PKR.
- **Currency Configuration** (`SOCurrencyConfig.tsx` `SOCurrencyConfigDialog`, the order number beside the title): Customer pays (the amount and the currency, read-only on an order from a quote); You receive ("≈ ₨…" when the account is in base, the order's amount when it is in the order's currency, with "Base equivalent ≈ ₨… PKR" under it) and the account picker (`components/currency/SOCurrencyFields.tsx` `AccountPicker`: flag, account, currency code); Rate (Daily market rate | Corporate rate, the rate card with "Nov 23, 2026 · order date", or "· from quote Q-…", then the lock chip and "locks when the order is confirmed", or "since the order was confirmed"). The amber note is gone; the lock line says the same. Picking another currency drops an account in the old one for 1010. The previewed total follows the rate as well as the currency, since fixed rule amounts do (below). Activity logs an account change as "You receive: 1010 · JPMorgan Operating → 1040 · Global FX Settlement" (`SOSidebar.tsx` `handleCurrencyChange`).
- **Locking.** Draft: unlocked. Confirmed, i.e. any status after Draft (submitted for review, released, shipped, …): locked. That is the point the configuration already turned read-only, so the edit guard and the version prompts are unchanged (`so/soCurrency.ts` `soRateLocked`, `soLockTitle`, `SO_LOCK_EVENT`).
- **Orders from a quote.** They start with the quote's currency, rate, rate type and pay-to account. The currency stays fixed; as a Draft the rate type, a corporate rate and the account can change, and they lock when the order is confirmed (until now such an order was locked from the start). `so/store.tsx` `createSO` reads what a conversion hands over, defensively: `payToAccount` (or a bare `settlementCurrency`), and the rate choice as `rateChoice` or `quoteRateChoice` ("quote" keeps the quote's rate, "today" takes today's of the same type; `SOFromQuoteFields`). Nothing hands it over yet: the Quotes app has no route into this one (its Convert to Sales Order stays in Quotes), so the seeded quote refs stand in. Changing a from-quote Draft's Requested On date no longer re-takes the rate, so the quote's rate stays (`SOSidebar.tsx`).
- **Version rate choice** in the map's words (`components/currency/VersionRateChoice.tsx`): "Keep version {n}'s rate" / "Use today's rate" and "Keep version {n}'s rate or use today's?", each with its rate; today's keeps its Daily market rate | Corporate rate switch.
- **Pricing rules in base.** A rule has no currency of its own: fixed amounts and value thresholds are PKR (`currency/soFx.ts` `RULE_AMOUNT_CURRENCY = BASE_CURRENCY`). A fixed amount converts to the order's currency at the order's own rate (`ruleAmountFactor(fx)` is 1 / rate), so it follows the order's rate type; it was USD at the order date's daily market rate cross rate. Seeded amounts written in USD are restated at the map's ×280 (`RULE_USD_RESTATE_FACTOR`): "Lighting Package Volume" is ₨56,000 off per lightbar (was $200; `so/pricingRulesData.ts`), and the library's templates and seeded rules (`so/PricingRulesLibrary.tsx`) read ₨210,000 / ₨700,000 / ₨840,000 / ₨2,800,000 where they read $750 / $2,500 / $3,000 / $10,000. The create form shows "Currency  PKR · base currency  locked" read-only, labels Amount (PKR) and Min/Max Order Value (PKR) with a ₨ prefix, and swaps the Value-Based "$" icon for a banknote. Rule chips read "₨56,000.00 (≈ $201.06)" (`components/currency/useRuleMoney.ts`). The line configurator works a fixed rule out as an amount per unit rather than a percentage, shown "₨56,000/unit" (`quote-creation/LineItemConfigModal.tsx` `ruleLineAmount`), and the library and the manage modal read "₨…" tiers as fixed amounts (`pricingRulesData.ts` `isFixedAmountText`). Percentages and quantity tiers don't change. The SO rule book has only quantity tiers, so no value threshold is checked on an order; the library's value-based templates are display only.
- **Printed order.** It prints in the order's currency, the total labelled with the code, "Total (USD)", and "All amounts in United States Dollars (USD)." under it (`so/soDocumentData.ts` `docCurrencyLabels`, used by `so/PrintSalesOrder.tsx`, the `so/SendToCustomerModal.tsx` preview and `components/shared/pdfDocument.tsx`). The name comes from the currency list ("United States Dollar"), so it reads "United States Dollars" where the map writes "US Dollars".

**Conflicts with earlier decisions (the map wins):**

- §3a: the toggle and the Customer Currency view are gone; the page is always in base.
- §3b (Mahreen, 20 Nov): an order from a quote was locked from the start; now its Draft is unlocked apart from the currency. The amber note ("The rate is set on the order date and locks once the order leaves Draft …") gives way to the map's lock line. The version choice was "Use locked rate from V{n}" / "Apply current rate".
- §5: "You receive" was any currency; now it is an account in PKR or the order's currency. Fixed rule amounts were USD at the daily market rate cross rate, so changing only the rate never moved the total; now they are PKR at the order's rate, so a new rate (a type switch, a typed corporate rate, today's rate on a version) moves what the customer pays on an order with a fixed-amount rule.

## 5. Data decisions

- **Customer currencies.** The design's customers are all US agencies, priced in USD. Six are seeded with another default currency so the listing shows orders in different currencies (`soFx.ts` `CUSTOMER_CURRENCIES`): Lakewood Community Hospital EUR, Summit Regional Medical Center GBP, Pacific Northwest Ambulance CAD, Heartland Air Ambulance AED, Coastal Search & Rescue JPY, and Valley Health Systems PKR (the base currency). Any other customer trades in USD. Change the map to change the demo.
- **Seeded orders** in another currency are restated once at the daily market rate on their order date (`store.tsx` `inCustomerCurrency`): line prices, freight, the order total, linked documents and the total quoted in the generated description. Each order is stamped with that rate. USD orders keep the design's numbers, so SO-000-000-000 matches the audit's screenshot ($132,186).
- **New orders** take the customer's currency and the daily market rate on the order date (`stampSoRate`); an order made from a quote with a rate keeps the quote's currency and rate (`createSO`), and its configuration is locked to the quote's (§3b). Catalog list prices, written in USD, convert at the same rate.
- **Pricing-rule flat amounts** ("$200 off per lightbar") stay written in USD, as Partners and Purchase Orders treat them, and convert at the daily market rate cross rate on the order's rate date (`ruleAmountFactor`), not at the order's own rate, so changing only the rate never changes the total. The rule library's create form labels its amounts USD. *Superseded on 24 Nov (§3c): rules are in PKR, restated ×280 (₨56,000 off per lightbar), and a fixed amount converts at the order's own rate.*
- **Demo seeds written in USD** (one-time adjustments, the mock version history and change notes, the insights catalog samples, customer revenue in the hover card) are restated in the order's, customer's or base currency where they show.
- **One-time adjustments** live on the SO page, not the order record (the design's model), so a reopened demo order re-seeds them, restated in its currency. An imported order's adjustments are kept in the new currency when its currency changes.
- **Linked documents** keep their own currency when it differs (a document raised before the order's currency changed); totals convert them into the order's.
- **You receive** (`SalesOrder.settlementCurrency`) is PKR unless set in the Currency Configuration. A duplicate for the same customer keeps it; for another customer it starts from PKR. *Since 24 Nov (§3c) it is an account, `SalesOrder.payToAccount` (1010 by default), in PKR or the order's currency; `settlementCurrency` follows it.*
- **Version rates.** A version records the currency and rate it took when it is started, reverted to or left (`SOVersion` currency fields). Seeded versions have none and read the order's. A duplicate with all versions drops them, since the copy takes today's rate.
- **Import and export** carry each order's currency. An imported order with no currency takes its customer's default.

## 6. Running inside the shell

As for Partners and Purchase Orders, it lives in `src/embedding.ts`:

- The router basename is set in `routes.tsx`, and plain in-app link clicks go through the router.
- `components/so/AppLayout.tsx` hides the app's own sidebar and moves the Sales View / Approver View toggle into the header (`AppSidebar.tsx` `PersonaToggle`).
- New tabs open the shell's page, except the Partner Management tab (`openPartnerTab`, `/partners?…`), which posts its result back to the window that opened it and so opens this app's own page.
- `touchpoints/touchpoints.json` points at the new repo. The shell's Sales links (`/sales/sales-orders`, …) are unchanged.

## 7. Not in this step

- **Quotes** are their own touchpoint (audit pp. 11–12). "A converted quote keeps its currency and rate" is honoured by `createSO` when a quote's currency and rate are passed; the redesign has no convert-from-quote flow of its own.
- **Carrier rate cards** (`ShipmentMethodModal.tsx` carrier methods, "$45.00") are carriers' tariffs, not the order's freight charge. They keep `$`. The freight charged to the customer is in the order's currency.
- **Sourcing & Procurement** (`SourcingProductionTab.tsx`) lists purchase orders to vendors; those are Purchase Orders' amounts. They keep `$`.
- **The rule book** (`PricingRulesLibrary.tsx`, `pricingRulesData.ts`) is written in USD; its cards keep `$`, which is USD. *Since 24 Nov (§3c) it is in PKR, ₨. Customers' credit limits in the library's partner lists still read `$`; they are partner data, not rule amounts.*
- **Unused design code** keeps `$`: the legacy Invoicing tab (the redesign shows "coming soon"), `LineEditDrawer.tsx`, `CreatePricingRuleModal.tsx`.
- **The outdated-configuration demo** (`data/outdatedConfigs.ts`) quotes a USD customer's credit limit in `$`.
- **Gaps in the design itself:** the listing's Order Value comes from `summarizeSO`, which leaves out rules and one-time adjustments, so it differs from Order Summary's Grand Total for the same order. The sidebar's mock invoices also show on new orders.
- **Upstream baseline:** `tsc` reports 32 errors, all in the design's own code; the one test passes. Both are the same in `Sales-Order-V5` itself.
